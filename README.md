# Formula Racing Web

A free open-wheel racing game that runs in the browser: three circuits, twenty cars, qualifying, pit stops with tyre choice, tyre wear, DRS, team radio and a cockpit view with a working steering-wheel display.

Play: https://timurikramov218-wq.github.io/formula-racing-web-/

Controls: arrow keys or W A S D to drive, E for DRS, B to call a pit stop, C to change camera, Esc to pause. On a phone or tablet the controls appear on screen.

Team and driver names are fictional; the circuit layouts come from the open outlines of github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT licence).
